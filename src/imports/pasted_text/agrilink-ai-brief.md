The goal is to connect farmers directly with buyers while providing:
- Transparent/latest available mandi market prices
- AI-assisted price predictions
- Best market recommendations
- Buyer matching
- Crop selling
- Buyer requirements
- Orders
- Payments
- Notifications
- Messaging
- Voice/IVR access
- Multilingual support
- Low-data/offline support
- Secure farmer authentication
- Trust and fraud protection

This must look like a serious national-scale startup/product and not like a basic student project.
